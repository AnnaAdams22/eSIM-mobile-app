## App Store — Description

```
ESIM Global keeps you connected the moment you arrive. Buy a travel eSIM for your destination, install it in minutes, and go online without hunting for a local SIM card or paying roaming fees.

HOW IT WORKS
- Choose your destination from 148+ countries and regions.
- Pay securely and your eSIM is delivered instantly, right in the app.
- Install with a QR code or, on supported phones, a single tap.
- Your plan activates on first use in the destination, so you're ready the moment you land.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
- Watch your live data balance and the days you have left.
- Get a heads-up when your data runs low or your plan is about to expire.
- Top up your current plan any time — your remaining data and days carry over, nothing is lost.
- Keep all your eSIMs and your order history in one tidy list.

SIMPLE, SECURE PAYMENTS
- Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, handled securely by Stripe.
- Prices are shown clearly in euros. No hidden fees, no surprise roaming charges.

SIGN IN YOUR WAY
- Use your email, or continue with Google. Sign in with Apple is available on iPhone.

TRAVEL LIGHT
- No physical SIM to swap, no queues, no roaming bills — just data that works wherever you're headed.

Available in English, Russian, German and Spanish.

Questions? Reach our support team right from the app.
```
